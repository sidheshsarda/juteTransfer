Page Structure Summary

  • Level 1 — Filters: Company, Branch, Year, Month dropdowns
  • Level 1 — Monthly MR Table: All MRs for the selected filters. Click a row to load its transfer chain.
  • Level 2 — Transfer Chain Cards: Vertical stack of step cards (Step 1 = gate entry, Step 2+ = transfers). Each card shows company, date, % rate increase input, warehouse, summary stats.
  • Level 3 — Line Items Table: Inside each card, a table showing item quality, weight, original rate, new rate (calculated), and line amount. Totals row at bottom.
  • Actions: Save/Clear/Delete buttons on the editing step card.
